# Controlled pilot checklist

Start with five people. Review their results before inviting the remaining 25. Use fictional job adverts and personal email addresses. Do not include confidential workplace examples.

## Each participant

Record the device, OS, browser, date, language, flow and result. Record an issue ID for each failure. Keep participant addresses and private interview links out of Git.

1. Open the invitation on the phone. Read the privacy notice and give your own consent.
2. Check the microphone. Record and listen to the test clip. Confirm the sound is clear.
3. Answer the interview questions. Check and correct the transcript. Confirm that follow-ups refer to the answer.
4. During a test upload, briefly interrupt the connection, then restore it. Confirm the answer uploads once and progress remains intact. Check refresh recovery separately.
5. Submit the interview. Confirm submission succeeds once, the interview locks, and the confirmation email arrives.
6. The employer opens the dashboard, plays each video with sound, checks the transcript and report, and records a test decision. Confirm the employer notification arrives.
7. Record any unclear instruction, misleading feedback or point where help was needed.

Cover iPhone Safari and Android Chrome across the five testers. If an invitation arrives through WhatsApp, check its embedded browser and the switch to the normal browser. Browser resizing is not evidence of camera, microphone or mobile upload behaviour.

## Separate private practice check

Choose a role, answer one question, correct the text, read the feedback and retry. Confirm private practice does not appear in the employer dashboard. Try both typed and spoken answers across the group. Include Arabic and imperfect English without treating accent or grammar as scoring criteria.

## Expansion decision

Before the remaining 25, resolve any lost recording, failed consent, duplicate submission, wrong-account access, missing notification or unusable report. Review feedback usefulness with the first five. A completed technical pilot does not establish hiring validity or capacity for 1,000 simultaneous users.
