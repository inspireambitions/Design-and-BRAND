Important limits

This is a career-planning guide, not a psychometric test, qualification check, visa assessment, salary survey or promise of employment. Hotel titles and pay vary by country, property tier, service charge, accommodation and responsibility. Verify requirements and current pay information where you intend to work before making a major decision.

Hospitality career path questions

Can I use it if my hotel job title is not listed?

Yes. Type your own job title and the role you want. Choose the closest skills and add any skill that is missing.

Can it help me change hotel departments?

Yes. Choose your current role and a target in another department. The roadmap will focus on transferable skills, missing exposure and practical evidence you can build.

Does it guarantee a salary or promotion?

No. It helps you plan and prepare. It does not know an employer's private pay band or hiring decision. Verify current local information before negotiating or moving.

What if I want to leave hospitality?

Use the general AI Career Coach. It will help you translate hotel experience into another type of work without treating your past experience as wasted.
