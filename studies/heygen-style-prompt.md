# HeyGen Style Prompt — Short-Form Talking Head (Ali Abdaal-inspired)

Reusable preset for generating brand-consistent short-form videos in HeyGen.
Paste **Block 1** into HeyGen's video prompt / creation settings, and use
**Block 2** as the script template for every new topic. **Block 3** is the
post-edit checklist (done in CapCut/Submagic after export — HeyGen cannot do
these parts).

---

## Block 1 — Style prompt (paste into HeyGen)

```
VIDEO STYLE PRESET — apply to this and all future videos:

FORMAT
- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, highest resolution available.
- Target length: 30–60 seconds.

AVATAR & FRAMING
- Talking head, chest-up, centered horizontally, positioned slightly below
  center frame so the lower-middle third stays clear for captions.
- Natural hand gestures, energetic but not exaggerated.

BACKGROUND / SET
- Warm, cozy home-studio look: wood tones, bookshelves, soft warm lamp light,
  slightly blurred (shallow depth of field). Background darker than the face.
- Same background every video for brand consistency.

VOICE & DELIVERY
- Conversational, warm, fast-paced and energetic — like explaining something
  exciting to a friend. No filler words, no long pauses.
- Emphasize key words. Slight upward energy at the start of each sentence.
- Start speaking immediately at frame one — no intro pause.

CAPTIONS (if generated in HeyGen)
- Auto-captions ON: bold heavy sans-serif (Montserrat ExtraBold or similar),
  white text with strong black shadow/outline.
- 2–4 words on screen at a time, synced to speech, centered, lower-middle third.
```

## Block 2 — Script template (fill in per topic)

```
[HOOK — boldest claim of the video, max 12 words, starts mid-thought]
e.g. "The reason your videos get ignored has nothing to do with quality."

[PROMISE — one sentence: what the viewer gets by staying]
e.g. "Here are the 3 things that actually make people stop scrolling."

[POINT 1 — one short punchy idea, 2–3 sentences max]
[POINT 2 — same]
[POINT 3 — same]

[PAYOFF/CTA — one sentence takeaway + follow prompt]
e.g. "Fix the hook first — everything else is optional. Follow for part 2."

SCRIPT RULES:
- Short sentences. No "Hey guys", no "in this video", no warm-up.
- One idea per sentence. Cut every word that doesn't earn its place.
- Name a specific book/app/number in each point (gives the edit a cutaway).
- Punctuate for rhythm — HeyGen respects commas and full stops as beats.
```

## Block 3 — Post-edit checklist (CapCut/Submagic, after HeyGen export)

1. Punch-ins: alternate 100% / ~120% scale on sentence boundaries, hard cuts only.
2. Captions restyle (if not done in HeyGen): white bold + black shadow,
   2–4 words per phrase, **one yellow keyword per phrase**, pop-in bounce.
3. Cutaways: 1–2s screenshot/book cover/chart wherever a specific noun is
   spoken, with slow zoom.
4. Music: quiet lo-fi bed ~-20 dB under voice.
5. SFX: soft whoosh/pop on caption hits and cutaway entrances.
6. Hook check: bold claim audible AND readable within first 1.5 seconds.
